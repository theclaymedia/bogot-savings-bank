1893

A legacy of trust

One of the oldest banks in New Jersey

In 1893, local businessman William Smith wanted the workers at his Bogota Paper Mills to have a place of their own to bank. With New York banker Victor Ludwig, he opened Bogota Building and Loan on March 31 of that year. More than 130 years later, we are still here for the same reason.

  1. 1893Bogota Building and Loan is founded in Bergen County.
  2. 1941Renamed Bogota Savings and Loan Association.
  3. 1996Becomes a state mutual and rebrands as Bogota Savings Bank.
  4. 2021Merges with Gibraltar Bank, expanding across North Jersey.
  5. TodayEight branches, from Bergen County to the Jersey Shore.
